The features available depend on the plan subscribed to (see the price list provided to the Customer) and can be enabled individually by the Publisher (Paramètres > Modules and Paramètres > Licence, i.e. Settings > Modules and Settings > Licence).

All sums due are payable upon subscription and thereafter on each renewal date — no payment period is granted.

  • Annual payment by bank transfer, the only payment method — the year is paid in a single instalment, by bank transfer, against invoice.   • Adding users during a period — the Customer may at any time increase the number of users on its licence. Added users give rise to an additional invoice calculated pro rata temporis for the period remaining until the annual renewal date, payable when the users are added; on renewal, invoicing covers all active users.

The licence is renewed (expiry date, Paramètres > Licence) by the Publisher upon receipt of payment.

In accordance with the regulations applicable under French commercial law, any sum invoiced and remaining unpaid when due shall automatically, and without prior formal notice, give rise to a late-payment indemnity calculated on the basis of three times the French statutory interest rate in force, together with a fixed indemnity for recovery costs of €40.
