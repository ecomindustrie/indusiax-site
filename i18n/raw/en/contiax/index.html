Frequently asked questions

It's free, so what's the catch?

There isn't one. Contiax costs us money — not much today, more as the number of users grows — and we treat it as a brand-awareness budget: if it is useful to you, you will know that Indusiax exists and that our software is designed by an industrial company, for industrial companies. That is all we expect.

What happens to the photo of the card?

It is received, stripped of its metadata — including the GPS location —, read, then discarded. It is never written to disk on our side. Our reading provider, Mistral AI, keeps it for up to thirty days to detect abuse; we would rather say so than suggest it vanishes instantly from end to end.

Does it work on anything other than a card?

Yes. Contiax reads contact details, not a card format. An email signature, photographed on your computer screen, reads just as well as a business card — often better, because it is consistently formatted and no logo runs across the name. It has even become the most common case: you receive more signatures in a week than you collect cards at a trade show.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Contiax opens in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen to find it like an app, but nothing goes through an app store.

What if there is no signal in the exhibition hall?

Take the photo anyway. The card waits in your phone and is sent automatically as soon as the connection returns. We designed it precisely for trade shows.

How many cards can I scan?

Ten a day, no strings attached. Beyond that, leave a business email address and you move up to fifty. These limits protect the service from automated use.

In which languages?

The interface is available in French, English, German, Italian, Spanish and Dutch. The cards themselves are read whatever their language.

Try it with the card lying on your desk

There is always one. Thirty seconds is enough to see the result.
