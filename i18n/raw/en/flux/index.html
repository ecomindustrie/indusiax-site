Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most often

Do I need to be good at writing to use Flux-Link?

No. You say three sentences out loud in a short video, as if you were showing the machine to a customer. The AI turns your description into a structured, professional LinkedIn post, which you review and adjust before publishing.

How long does it take to publish a post?

About ten minutes a week is enough: a few photos already on your phone, a 20-second video, the AI does the writing, a quick review, one click. On LinkedIn, consistency is everything — Flux-Link makes it possible without hiring anyone.

Are my photos and voice recordings kept?

Your voice is transcribed on our servers in Europe — it is never sent to a third-party service. Once the post has been published, the photos and video are automatically deleted from our servers. Only the text of the post stays in your history.

Which languages does Flux-Link write in?

Four languages: French, English, Dutch and Italian. The interface, the transcription and the generated post all adapt to the language you choose — handy for companies that work on both sides of a border.

How do I connect to LinkedIn?

Through LinkedIn's official authorisation (OAuth): you click, you approve, and Flux-Link can post on your behalf. You can revoke the authorisation at any time from LinkedIn or from the app. No LinkedIn password ever passes through Flux-Link.
