Why Indusiax exists

Indusiax was not born in an incubator. It was born in a French industrial SME that was looking for software for its trade — and could not find any.

CRMs cut out for the tech sector, overpriced field tools, meeting minutes shipped off to servers on the other side of the world… So we built our own tools, through daily use, with our sales reps and our technicians. They run our business every day. Today, we are putting them in your hands.
