Why Vector exists

Our meetings are our own business: job sites, disputes, prices, staff. The kind of thing you do not send to an American cloud giant to be transcribed.

Every meeting-minutes tool we tested shipped our recordings off who knows where. Unacceptable when the subject is a confidential quote or an HR case. So we built our own: transcription runs on our own servers, in Europe, and nothing leaves. It is automatic minutes an industrial company can use with a clear conscience.
