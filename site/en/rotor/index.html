Who it's for

Built for independent inspectors and inspection SMEs.

The market

VGP only requires a “qualified person” (personne qualifiée).

No mandatory accreditation for routine inspection. That is what gave rise to a dense network of independent providers in France — and it is exactly for them that Rotor-FSM is designed.
