Why Stator exists

We were an industrial company before we were a software publisher. We spent a long time looking for a CRM that fitted our trade. All we found were bloated systems designed for tech companies or online retail.

None of them understood a six-figure machine quote, the workshop's sales rounds, the serial number installed at the customer's site, or the after-sales call that comes back three years later. So we built it ourselves — first for our own use, with our own sales reps, out in the field. Stator-CRM is that tool: the one we wish we had found.
