1. Purpose

These general terms and conditions (the “Terms of Use and Sale”, in French “CGU/CGV”) govern access to and use of the software for managing field interventions and drafting on-site expert reports, marketed under the name ROTOR FSM, provided as SaaS (Software as a Service) by Grégory DURAND — ECOM-INDUSTRIE (SIREN 521 070 284, the “Publisher”) to its business customers (the “Customer”). Any subscription to the Service constitutes full and unreserved acceptance of these Terms of Use and Sale.

2. Description of the Service

The Service is provided on a single-tenant architecture: each Customer has its own dedicated application instance and database. It enables the Customer's technician teams to create and track field interventions (referenced by A.R.C. number), to draft expert reports (technical data sheets, equipment photos, signatures), and to generate the corresponding documents. The Customer may configure the visual identity (logo, contact details) appearing on the documents issued to its own customers — this customisation does not affect the identity of the Publisher for the purposes of these terms. The number of users and the features available are set by the licence associated with the instance, in accordance with the plan subscribed to.

VAT (VAT not applicable — French VAT exemption, art. 293 B CGI, for as long as the Publisher falls under the basic VAT exemption scheme, “franchise en base”). The Customer chooses its number of users; the subscription amount is the unit price multiplied by that number. The Service is reserved for businesses; there is no monthly subscription.
  • Any price change is notified to the Customer at least 30 days before it takes effect.
  • Additional services — the subscription price does not include: user training sessions (optional), the migration and integration of the Customer's historical databases, the digitisation of Customer-specific job forms, the development of bespoke tools, or any other service outside the standard subscription. These services are performed at the Customer's request and are subject to a separate quote. They are invoiced only after the Customer's express acceptance of the quote, and are then added to the amount of the current subscription without altering its term or conditions.

All sums due are payable upon subscription and thereafter on each renewal date — no payment period is granted.

  • Annual payment by bank transfer, the only payment method — the year is paid in a single instalment, by bank transfer, against invoice. Annual payment entitles the Customer to a discount of two (2) months: twelve (12) months of access for the price of ten (10) months. The annual period, and its renewal, takes effect upon receipt of payment. Payment by credit or debit card is not offered for this Service.
  • Subscription process — the subscription is formalised by a quote issued in the Customer's name, then, following acceptance or a purchase order from the Customer, by an invoice paid by bank transfer. Access is opened upon receipt of payment.
  • Late payment — if payment is not made by the due date, the Publisher informs the Customer; if the situation is not remedied within 8 days, write access to the Service may be suspended (read-only, without deletion of data) until payment is made.
  • Adding users during a period — the Customer may at any time increase the number of users on its licence. Added users give rise to an additional invoice calculated pro rata temporis for the period remaining until the annual renewal date, payable when the users are added; on renewal, invoicing covers all active users.

The licence is renewed (expiry date, displayed in the application) by the Publisher upon receipt of payment.

In accordance with the regulations applicable under French commercial law, any sum invoiced and remaining unpaid when due shall automatically, and without prior formal notice, give rise to a late-payment indemnity calculated on the basis of three times the French statutory interest rate in force, together with a fixed indemnity for recovery costs of €40. No discount is granted for early payment.
