Why so cheap, and why so low-key

These two tools are not our core business. Our core business is four management software products for small and mid-sized industrial companies. Contiax and the NFC cards are a way in: they cost little because they are not there to make a margin, but so that an industrial company that did not know us finds out that we exist.

That is also why every NFC contact page carries a “Powered by Indusiax” line at the bottom, and why Contiax is free with no strings attached — no account to create, no advertising, no reselling of data. If either of them proves useful, you will know that our software is designed by someone from industry, for people in industry. That is all we expect in return.
